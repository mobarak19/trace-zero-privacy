FAQ

Questions, answered.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every clean and blur runs on your device. There's no account and no cloud processing.

Will my original photo or video be changed?

No. Trace Zero works on a copy and exports a cleaned result. Your original stays exactly as it was.

Will video quality be affected?

Turn on Preserve Original Quality to avoid re-encoding when possible. If re-encoding is needed, the result depends on the format and quality you choose in Export settings.

Does cleaning audio change the sound?

No. MP3, WAV and M4A files are cleaned without re-encoding. Other formats are converted, and the app tells you when that happens.

Is blurring the same as metadata cleaning?

No. Metadata cleaning removes hidden information stored inside a file. Redaction changes what's visible in the picture itself. Automatic detection can miss things, so always check the result before sharing.

Can I clean files from other apps?

Yes, with Trace Zero Pro. In Photos, Files or any app, tap Share and choose Trace Zero. Metadata is removed on your device, then you can share or save the clean copies.

Can metadata come back after sharing?

Yes. Some apps and services create new metadata when they import, convert or re-save a file. For the most privacy, check the final file before sharing.

Does Trace Zero work on iPad and Mac?

Yes. Trace Zero runs on iPhone, iPad and Mac, with a layout built for each screen.

Does Trace Zero show ads or track me?

No. There are no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking in the app.

Can I use Pro on another device?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple Account and tap Restore to unlock Pro there.
