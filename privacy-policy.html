Trace Zero

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

1. Overview

Trace Zero is a privacy-focused media utility designed to help you remove supported metadata from photos, videos, and audio files and, where available, redact visual information such as faces, license plates, and documents.

We designed Trace Zero around an on-device processing model. Your selected media is intended to remain on your device while Trace Zero performs its cleaning or redaction operations.

Privacy by design: Trace Zero does not require you to upload your personal photos, videos, or audio files to a Trace Zero server to perform its core cleaning and redaction features.

2. Information We Process

Trace Zero may access information contained within media that you explicitly select for processing.

Photos

Depending on your selected privacy settings, Trace Zero may process supported photo metadata such as GPS/location information, dates, device information, EXIF information, and other supported metadata.

Videos

Depending on your selected settings, Trace Zero may process supported video metadata including location, date, device information, software information, descriptions, and other supported metadata.

Audio

Depending on your selected settings, Trace Zero may process supported audio metadata such as metadata, location, date, and embedded artwork.

Redaction

When you use a redaction feature, Trace Zero may analyze the selected media locally to identify supported visual regions such as faces, license plates, documents, or other supported areas that you choose to redact.

For video redaction you may also choose to replace or remove the audio track. If you do, Trace Zero reads the audio file you select through Apple's file picker and processes it locally.

3. Photos and Media Library Access

Trace Zero requests access to your Photos library only when necessary for a feature you initiate, such as selecting a photo or video for cleaning or redaction.

Trace Zero does not automatically scan your entire Photos library. You control which media you provide to the app through Apple's system-provided media selection interfaces.

If selected media is stored in iCloud and is not currently available locally, Apple's Photos framework may need to download the selected original before Trace Zero can process it.

4. On-Device Processing

Trace Zero's core cleaning and redaction workflows are designed to process selected media locally on your device.

This means that your selected media is not intentionally uploaded to Trace Zero's servers as part of the normal metadata-cleaning or redaction workflow.

Processing may create temporary files or cleaned copies locally on your device. These files are used to complete the requested operation and may be removed according to the app's implementation and operating-system storage behavior.

5. iCloud

Trace Zero may interact with Apple's Photos framework when you select media from your Photos library.

If Apple needs to download an iCloud-stored original to your device, that transfer is handled by Apple's systems and is subject to Apple's own terms and privacy practices.

Trace Zero does not operate Apple's iCloud service.

6. Redaction and Computer Vision

Trace Zero may use on-device computer vision technologies to identify supported visual content for redaction.

Depending on the feature, this may include detection of faces, license plates, documents, or other supported visual regions.

Detection systems are not guaranteed to identify every object, person, plate, document, or sensitive area. You are responsible for reviewing the result before sharing or publishing the processed media.

7. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

Trace Zero is designed so that its core media-processing workflows do not require us to collect:

  • Your photos
  • Your videos
  • Your audio files
  • The original media files you select
  • The cleaned media files produced by the app
  • GPS coordinates contained in your media
  • Photo or video metadata for advertising purposes

This list describes the intended behavior of the core application. Platform services, purchases, crash reporting, analytics, or other optional services may process limited technical information as described below.

8. Purchases and Subscriptions

Trace Zero may offer free and paid functionality, including Pro features or other in-app purchases.

Purchases are processed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Trace Zero does not receive or store your complete payment card information.

Apple may process purchase-related information according to Apple's own privacy policies and terms.

Trace Zero checks your purchase status directly with Apple's StoreKit on your device to unlock Pro features. We do not maintain user accounts and do not receive your name, email address, or payment details from Apple.

9. Data Stored on Your Device

Trace Zero stores a small amount of information locally on your device so the app can work as expected:

  • Your settings, such as the metadata categories you choose to remove and your export format and quality preferences.
  • An activity history that lists what you processed, for example the number of files, the type of media (photo, video, or audio), whether metadata cleaning or redaction was used, which categories were removed, and the date. The history does not contain your photos, videos, audio, or file names.
  • Usage counters used to enforce free daily limits, and the state of your Pro purchase as reported by Apple.

This information stays on your device. You can delete individual history entries or clear the whole history from within the app. Deleting the app removes the data stored by the app.

Free Usage Limits

Trace Zero may provide limited free usage of cleaning and redaction functionality.

The app may locally track usage counters necessary to enforce those limits, such as the number of free cleaning or redaction operations available during a period.

These counters are not intended to contain your media files or the contents of your media.

10. Notifications

If you allow notifications, Trace Zero may show a local notification when a long-running video job finishes while the app is in the background. These notifications are created on your device. They do not involve a Trace Zero server, and Trace Zero does not send push notifications. You can turn notifications off at any time in iOS Settings.

11. Analytics and Diagnostics

Trace Zero does not include third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising software, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

If you have chosen to share analytics and crash data with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may make aggregated and anonymized diagnostic information available to us. We do not receive your media through this process.

If you contact support or send feedback from the app, it opens a pre-filled email in your own mail app. That email includes your app version, device model, and iOS version to help us troubleshoot. You can review and edit the message before sending it. We use the information you send only to respond to you.

If we add analytics or crash-reporting services in the future, we will update this policy before or when they take effect.

12. Advertising

Trace Zero does not show advertisements and does not share your information with advertisers. If this changes in a future version, we will update this policy.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application. Because Trace Zero is designed around local processing, much of the sensitive media-processing workflow occurs directly on your device rather than on a Trace Zero server.

No software or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

14. Data Retention

Trace Zero does not intentionally retain copies of your original personal media on Trace Zero servers as part of its normal cleaning or redaction workflow.

Cleaned or redacted files may remain on your device if you choose to save or export them.

You are responsible for managing saved files through Apple's Photos app, Files app, or other storage locations where you choose to keep them.

15. Children's Privacy

Trace Zero is not designed specifically for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the core media-processing functionality of the app.

16. Third-Party Services

Trace Zero may rely on Apple frameworks and services, including Photos, StoreKit, Vision, and operating-system functionality.

When you open the Privacy Policy or Terms of Use inside the app, the page is loaded from our website, and the site's host may receive standard connection information such as your IP address.

If additional third-party services are introduced, their processing practices may be governed by their respective privacy policies.

17. Your Choices

You can control access to Trace Zero through your device settings.

  • Manage Photos access through iOS Settings.
  • Delete media you no longer want to keep.
  • Choose which media to provide to Trace Zero.
  • Choose which metadata categories to remove.
  • Choose whether to save cleaned or redacted copies.
  • Manage purchases through your Apple Account.

18. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Trace Zero's features, services, or legal requirements change.

The updated policy will be published with a revised "Last updated" date.
